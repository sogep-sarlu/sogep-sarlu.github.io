01 · About us

One supplier for all your equipment needs

SOLUTIONS GLOBALES D'ÉQUIPEMENTS ET DE PRESTATIONS SARLU (SOGEP-SARLU) is a company incorporated under Guinean law, specialising in general trading, purchasing and resale, import and distribution of goods and equipment.

We supply everything a business may need in equipment and furniture: vehicle and generator parts, pumping equipment, protective equipment, building materials, office supplies and office fit-out. Our ranges are only a selection: any other item is supplied on request.

Our method is simple: understand your requirement, send you a clear offer, then deliver with fully compliant commercial and tax documents.

Our mission

To make procurement simpler for businesses operating in Guinea: one point of contact, clear offers and flawless paperwork.

04 · Services and terms

How we work

Recurring supply

For consumables you need every month: filters, PPE, supplies. A standard list agreed with you and scheduled orders.

Special orders

Branded parts, items outside the catalogue, unusual quantities: sourcing by manufacturer's part number and import if needed.

Delivery to site

To your offices, warehouses or sites, in Conakry and upcountry. Installation of blinds and curtains on request.

How to order

  1. 01Request

    You send your list or request for quotation by email or WhatsApp.

  2. 02Pro forma offer

    We send you a detailed pro forma invoice, valid for 30 days.

  3. 03Purchase order

    You confirm the offer with your purchase order (PO).

  4. 04Delivery

    We deliver with a delivery note signed on receipt.

  5. 05Invoicing

    We issue the final invoice, compliant with SYSCOHADA.

06 · Ethics and human rights

Human rights and the fight against modern slavery

SOGEP-SARLU is committed to complying with human rights laws and policies, and to having no link with forced labour, slavery or human trafficking, in its own operations or in its supply chain.

Knowledge of the rules

SOGEP-SARLU, its staff and affiliates know the laws and policies relating to human rights.

Accurate information

Our answers to due diligence questionnaires are accurate and complete at the time they are provided.

No convictions

Neither SOGEP-SARLU nor its directors, agents or intermediaries have been convicted of any offence related to slavery or trafficking.

Compliance with the law

Our staff comply with human rights laws in carrying out every order.

Zero forced labour

We buy no goods from producers or manufacturers that use forced labour.

Committed suppliers

Our contracts with key subcontractors and suppliers include equivalent commitments.

Transparency

We inform our client as soon as we become aware of a breach related to their order.

Traceability

We keep records that trace the supply chain of every order.

Right to audit

With reasonable notice, our clients may inspect these records and meet our staff.

We sign our clients' human rights and anti-slavery clauses and complete your supplier questionnaires on request.
